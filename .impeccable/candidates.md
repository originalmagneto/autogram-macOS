MECHANISM: the Mac itself becomes the signing instrument; a qualified signature is applied locally (Finder, Safari on state portals, an iPhone NFC tap) with every commit confirmed, no Java window, no open port.
SCENE: a Slovak Mac user (founder, accountant, freelancer, advocate) at a desk, in daylight, who has fought D.Signer/D.Bridge, reader drivers and portal errors, and needs to sign a PDF or submit a form now.
CULTURAL HOME: Central European design culture (Czechoslovak graphic modernism, security printing, the eID card itself) meeting Mac pro-app craft.
RUT: blue/green trust SaaS with shield icons, gradient glow and a MacBook-framed screenshot; opposite rut: dark neon crypto/security. Literal name reading (heraldic chevrons, 7 locks) gets at most one slot.

1. Security printing (guilloche, intaglio, microtext, OVD on the eID card and banknotes): the signature's real ancestor is anti-forgery craft; mechanism = unforgeable mark made locally. Family: security print.
2. Seal and cord ritual (sealing wax, notarial tricolor cord through eyelets, embossed dry seal, sealing tape): the physical ancestors of KEP and literally the security elements ZaKo detects. Family: physical sealing.
3. Czechoslovak graphic modernism (1960s-80s poster school, Slovak stamp and book design, bold flat colour, cut-paper geometry): local, confident, not a state look, not papery. Family: graphic tradition.
4. Mac pro-app interface grammar (Logic, Final Cut, Liquid Glass inspectors, HUDs): the product's own native language shown at full craft. Family: interface.
5. Ticket validation / komposter punch (tram ticket stamped in the validator, the clack that commits): the ritual of committing a document with one decisive act. Family: ritual/object.
6. Philatelic cancellation and postmark (perforations, cancellation waves, date rings): a mark that proves when and by whom, like a qualified timestamp. Family: postal graphics.
7. Heraldic ordinaries (chevron as a bold field division, seven stacked chevrons as progress): the name read literally, used as a pure geometric banner system. Family: heraldry (the one literal slot).
