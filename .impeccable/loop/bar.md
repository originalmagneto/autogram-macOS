# Bar: apple.com/macos (macOS 27 Golden Gate page), viewed at 1440x900

Mechanisms a critic can check by looking. Each is translated to Chevron7's world
(midnight ground, one amber light), never copied as Apple's clothes.

1. **The product moves, it is never a still.** Every feature block below the
   hero shows the real interface in motion (looping video of the UI doing the
   thing: typing, results appearing, a panel opening). Check: at least 3 of the
   feature blocks contain a playing video or a real UI animation, not a static
   screenshot.

2. **Motion media carries its own control.** Every autoplaying tile has a small
   pill control (progress dots for the gallery position plus a pause/play
   button), bottom-centre, on a translucent dark pill. Check: every looping video
   has a visible pause control; nothing moves that the visitor cannot stop.

3. **Features live in a horizontal gallery of large rounded media tiles.**
   Tiles are about 70 to 85 percent of the content width, radius about 28px,
   the next tile peeks at the right edge, with circular prev/next arrow buttons
   under the gallery at the right. Check: one section is such a gallery with a
   visible peek of the next tile and working arrows.

4. **Caption under the media, lead-in in bold, same paragraph.** Under each
   tile: one paragraph, max about 36 characters wide per column, starting with a
   bold sentence ("Just ask Siri AI.") followed by regular grey text. Check:
   captions sit below media, bold lead-in plus grey body in one paragraph, no
   separate title element.

5. **Headline scale is constant and big; everything else is small.** Section
   headlines are two short lines at about 48 to 56px, body copy about 17px, and
   nothing in between except the 12px-ish captions. Three type sizes per
   section at most. Check: count the distinct type sizes in any one section.

6. **Chapters are marked by a change of ground, not by rules or cards.** The
   page switches the whole section background tone (Apple: dark to light grey)
   to start a new chapter. Check: at least one full-bleed tonal change between
   chapters; no horizontal divider lines doing that job.

7. **Small delights on direct manipulation.** Interactive things respond with
   a physical feel: buttons press, the media pill animates its progress bar,
   hovering media brings it forward slightly. Check: hover and press states on
   every control and media tile, and a progress indicator that fills over time.
